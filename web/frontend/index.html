Agentic OSINT Harness

Pick a company, a person, or a claim, or upload a document and it will check the main claim the document makes. The agent plans lines of enquiry, searches the live web, reads what it finds, grades every publisher and every assertion on the Admiralty scale, weighs competing hypotheses by what disconfirms them, and reflects before committing to a verdict with a calibrated confidence. Every citation traces back to a document it actually retrieved, live, while you watch. There is no offline stand-in on this path. Source on GitHub.

The agent reads your document, picks out the main factual claim it makes, and checks that claim against sources it finds on the live web. The document never counts as evidence for its own claim. Its text is sent to the model provider and the claim to a search API, so please don't upload anything confidential. The list of your documents is kept in this browser only.
